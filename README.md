## Welcome to my GH profile

I'm Javier Palau, a MSc Physics graduate interested in machine learning and physical simulations with Python and differentiable simulations. I hope that with the following projects you can get a taste of my skillset and interests:

 - [Neural Operator based superresolution of Magnetohydrodynamics snapshots of turbulence](https://github.com/javinukem/super_resolution_turbulence_FNO):
- [Neural Operator based superresolution of Magnetohydrodynamics snapshots of turbulence](https://github.com/javinukem/super_resolution_turbulence_FNO):
  - In this work I explored Neural Operators as a means to superresolve slices of a MHD simulation of turbulence.
  - I used FNOs as the problem's main cause of symmetry comes from the Kolmogorov physical laws, which explain the shape and size of the Fourier spectrum in turbulence scenarios.
  - **Skills used:** Python, PyTorch, Neural Operators, PINNs.

- [Machine learning enhanced MHD simulations of the Interstellar Medium with Solvers-in-the-loop](https://github.com/javinukem/master-thesis-hybrid-ml-simulation-ism):
   - In this work I explored Solvers-in-the-loop with differentiable simulations in JAX to correct the simulation in-situ.
   - I used a time-interval-independent, gradient-free CNN as a model which incorporates the symmetries needed for the simulation.
   - **Skills used**: Python, JAX, Differentiable simulations, PINNs, model evaluation, CFD
 - [Neutral buoyancy ocean trajectories simulations](https://github.com/javinukem/neutral_buoyancy_ocean_trayectories_sim):
   - Part of my Bachelor's thesis, where we simulated ocean trajectories which respect a neutral buoyancy condition.
   - The goal was to explore the helix behavior, occurring when this trajectories are closed.
